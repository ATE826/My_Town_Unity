Urban Street Props: Game-Ready Pack
Created by Rozity

Technical Details:

-Textures: 2048x2048 (PBR)

-Format: FBX & .unitypackage

-Colliders: Included in all prefabs.

Pipeline Support:
This package includes support for two Unity render pipelines:

URP Folder: Materials and prefabs for Universal Render Pipeline.

Built-in Folder: Materials and prefabs for Standard renderer.

Note: HDRP is not natively included but URP materials can be converted.

Installation:
-Import Urban_Street_Props_v1.unitypackage into your Unity project.

-Open the folder Urban_Props_Pack_Rozity / Prefabs.

-Choose the folder that matches your project's render pipeline (URP or Built-in).

-Drag the prefabs into your scene.
